## Patterns to cite in interview

1. Robot mix across cases: FANUC CRX (most common), UR (10e/20), Doosan, one industrial FANUC M-710iC, Epson (Grupo Kopar) — multi-vendor streaming is the product, not a feature.
2. Recurring outcome shape: <20-mo ROI, operators reallocated, weeks-not-months deployment — speed of deployment is the brand promise; motion stack must be configuration-driven, not taught.
3. JD apps map 1:1 to shipped products: palletizing ✓, finishing ✓ (Rapid Series Sanding), extruding ✓ (JMC/Bright IA adjacent), packing ✓ (McAlpine/Niederrhein).
4. Quebec density: Polykar, Produlith, Spector, Fabridor = Montreal-area references; bilingual support matters.
