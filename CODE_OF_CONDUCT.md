# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for us as individuals

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Technical disagreement is not misconduct

Arguing that a design is wrong, that a benchmark is misleading, or that an
abstraction leaks is the work. Do it about the code, with reasons. Attacking the
person rather than the argument is what this document is about.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
will take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. They may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when
appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by contacting a
maintainer directly through their GitHub profile, or by opening a GitHub
security advisory (which is private) if a public issue would expose the
reporter. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly,
and the privacy and security of the reporter respected.

### Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning, with clarity about the nature of
   the violation. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — consequences for continued behaviour, and a period of no
   interaction with those involved.
3. **Temporary ban** from any interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** for a pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression.
